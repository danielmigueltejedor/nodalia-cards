## Provenance and attempts

Exact published 2.2.10, beta.1 and RC1 assets; seven measured rounds and two warmups per version/engine. Apple M4, 16 GiB, arm64, macOS 27.2, Node v24.14.0, pnpm 11.17.0, Playwright 1.63.0. Chromium 153.0.8010.12; WebKit/iPhone 26.6. Engines are separate. The combined measured fixture SHA256 is `fffc84a0fc90814a4486adc2db4221be0b388b12e2ca06e703f67e363e7f3a30`.

Accepted composition: 8,505 samples, zero measured-engine errors and 147 explicit unsupported generation-2 operations. The [first failed attempt](rc1-reference/original-failed.json) retains 8,233 samples and its three iPhone WebKit errors (legacy Gauge import/setup, navigation timeout, later module import failure). A separate [100 fresh-page legacy Gauge import probe](rc1-reference/nodalia-rc-import-probe.json) ([script](rc1-reference/nodalia-rc-import-probe.mjs)) produced zero errors; the intermittent failure cause is unconfirmed. No runtime correction is claimed for it.

The [complete iPhone repeat](rc1-reference/retry/2.2.10-vs-3.0.0-beta.1-vs-3.0.0-rc.1.json) reruns every version, workload and round with unchanged fixture/config/system/browser/asset hashes. The other two browsers are deliberately excluded at launch in that raw attempt; those two launch errors remain recorded there. Composition retains all first-run Chromium/desktop WebKit measurements and replaces all iPhone measurements, never favourable individual samples. `metadata.engineRetry` preserves both timestamps, original errors, orchestration hash and retry commit. See the [repeat protocol](README.md#whole-engine-retry). Idle sleep was prevented during the repeat with a process-bound assertion; no permanent system setting changed.

Firefox reference performance is explicitly unavailable on this Mac. Linux Firefox compatibility passed. This exception cannot produce stable 3.0.0 performance evidence. Working-tree dirtiness records the pre-existing untracked duplicate test; measured harness files were committed and clean.

## Acceptance

RC acceptance is complete under the explicit three-engine policy, with exact published asset integrity, complete workload coverage, recorded negative results and four-engine Linux functionality. Architecture/features/API/YAML/distribution are frozen; stabilization only. Stable promotion still requires four-engine exact stable-asset evidence on a provisioned controlled runner and material-regression reassessment. No raw attempts or unfavourable samples were discarded. [Archive checksums](rc1-reference/SHA256SUMS) preserve both attempts and the probe.
